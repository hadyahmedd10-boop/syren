cluster reinforces a single page instead of creating duplicate pages that compete
with each other (cannibalisation).

> Console and the Bing Webmaster keyword tool before these targets are treated as
> final. No volume numbers are asserted here.

### Rules of the map

- Each URL above has exactly **one** primary keyword. Never give two URLs the same
  primary keyword.
- Supporting pages (`/events`, `/experiences`, `/guides`, `/home`, the footer and
  the individual event pages) **link** to these URLs but do not re-target their
  primary keywords in their own titles/H1s.
- `/transfers` owns the transfer intent (how to get there, private transfers to a
  Pyramids concert). The festival landing pages link to it rather than competing
  for it.

### Intents covered by the copy (not separate URLs)

Where to stay · how to get tickets · how to get there · is it safe · what to pack ·
how long to stay · what else to do in Egypt. These are answered inside the existing
pages and their FAQ sections, not split into new URLs.
